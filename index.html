Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started

How long does it take to build a website?

Typical timelines range from 1–2 weeks for a starter site to 4–6 weeks for a full e-commerce platform. The exact timeline depends on complexity, content readiness, and how quickly feedback is provided during the review stages.

Do I need to have content ready?

Not at all. I can help craft your content, suggest structure, and even write placeholder copy during the design phase. However, having your branding assets (logo, colors, images) ready upfront helps speed things up.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Absolutely. Every site I build is mobile-first and fully responsive. It will look and function perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops. Mobile responsiveness is tested thoroughly before launch.

Do you offer hosting or domain services?

I don't sell hosting directly, but I help you choose a reliable provider and handle the full setup and deployment. I recommend options starting as low as $5/month that work great for small to medium business sites.

What if I need changes after the site is live?

Every package includes a support period with free minor updates. For ongoing maintenance, I offer affordable monthly retainer plans that cover updates, backups, security monitoring, and priority support.

Can I see examples of your previous work?

Of course! Browse the Projects section above to see real sites I've built. Each project card links to the live site so you can explore them firsthand. I'm also happy to share more relevant examples during our call.
